Why MoCHi

Capability, with room to make it yours.

MoCHi puts model capability into a workspace you can configure around the task. Bring your own provider keys, choose the models that fit, keep context close, and move from research to creation, analysis and development in one place.

Provider choice

Use your own provider keys and choose the models that fit the task.

Memory & context

Search & files

Projects & workspace

Organize ongoing work in customizable project spaces.

Voice & images

Use voice and image tools when the task calls for more than text.

Charts & creation

Turn data and ideas into charts and other useful outputs.
