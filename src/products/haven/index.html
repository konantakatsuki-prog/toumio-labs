Haven

Natural voice for AI interaction. A voice system being developed for expressive AI speech and conversational experiences.

Coming soon

Why voice

Conversation should have texture.

Voice changes the pace, feeling and shape of an interaction. Haven is an ongoing exploration of the systems, models and design decisions that help AI speech feel more present without becoming distracting.

What we're exploring

Expressiveness

How can a voice carry intention, warmth and restraint?

Responsiveness

How should speech behave when a conversation moves quickly?

Control

How can people shape a voice without having to over-specify it?

Infrastructure

What makes a voice system useful for the products built on top of it?

Haven is in development. We will share more as the work becomes ready.
